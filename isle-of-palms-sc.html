Building on Isle of Palms

Salt air is the first thing to plan for on Isle of Palms. It corrodes cheap fixtures and fasteners, so for landscape lighting near the coast we use solid brass, copper or marine-grade aluminum. Plastic kit fixtures corrode.

The island is also a sea turtle nesting beach. Turtle season runs from May 1 to October 31, and during it the city requires decorative lights that can be seen from the beach to be shielded or turned off from sunset to sunrise. Any lighting plan for a beachfront lot has to be designed around that, and the city has the final say.

Raised houses change the layout too. The ground level is where an outdoor shower belongs, and we plumb them hot and cold, because a hot shower gets used after the beach in October. Around a pool, travertine stays cool underfoot, which is why it is a common choice for a pool deck. For the lawn, artificial turf earns its money where grass keeps failing, and an irrigation system decides whether the planting lives.

Wild Dunes and the Rest of the Island

The City of Isle of Palms requires new hard landscaping, including walkways, driveways, pool surrounds and ground-level patios, to be built from pervious materials, and it caps impervious cover on a lot. Removing a tree of 8 inches or more, palms included, needs a city permit.

Inside Wild Dunes, the community’s own architectural review board adds more:

  • Fences around a whole lot or in a front yard are generally not approved. A small child or pet area can be, in 48-inch black aluminum with square pickets.
  • Artificial turf is allowed only inside a rear pool fence or a rear child or pet fence.
  • Driveways are concrete, brick or stone pavers, or asphalt. Crushed shell and crushed stone are not permitted.
  • Fire: no wood-burning fires are permitted outdoors, so a fire pit or fireplace there has to be gas, and a propane fireplace needs board approval.
  • Lighting stays low, shielded and warm white, with outdoor lights off at midnight apart from low step lights.

Rules as published when we checked in 2026. Guidelines change, and the board or town always has the final say, so we confirm the current rules for your property before a design is final.

Every Service We Offer, Available On Isle of Palms

The projects on this page are only part of what we do. We offer all of our services on Isle of Palms, designed by Doug and Zach and built by our own crew. From the first site visit to the final walkthrough, here’s how a project works.

  • Landscaping: landscape design and installation, plants and trees, sod, artificial turf, irrigation, drainage and grading, landscape lighting, mulch and bed edging, fountains and water features
  • Hardscaping: patios and pavers, concrete and driveways, retaining walls, fire pits, pool decks
  • Outdoor Structures: pergolas, pavilions, outdoor kitchens, outdoor fireplaces, outdoor showers

Worth knowing before you start

  • Landscape lighting: Near the coast we use solid brass, copper or marine-grade aluminum. Plastic kit fixtures corrode.
  • Outdoor showers: No permit is needed for an outdoor shower. Some HOAs want approval when it is visible from the street, and we handle that.
  • Concrete and driveways: Poured concrete is about $12 per square foot. Salt-void, tabby and stamped finishes are all options and look good for the money. Poured in-house, at the thickness you paid for, with the mesh, rebar and joint spacing done properly.
  • Artificial turf: From $13 per square foot, including the base preparation. For dogs we use a high-permeability backing, antimicrobial infill and a well-draining base.
  • Outdoor kitchens: Granite counters and stainless appliances hold up best here.

Small jobs are welcome too. There’s no minimum job size.

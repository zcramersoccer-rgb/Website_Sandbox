Isle of Palms Outdoor Living, Bright and Coastal

Cramers Landscaping is a family company. Doug Cramer has spent more than 30 years building landscapes in the Lowcountry, and his son Zach is our licensed residential builder. Doug and Zach design every project themselves, and our own crew builds it, including the gas, electrical and plumbing.

The pool pavilion pictured here is our Isle of Palms project. It is very bright and coastal: a modern coastal pavilion with a tabby fireplace, on a travertine pool deck.

The Space Under the House

This Isle of Palms house is raised up. For the same client we converted the area under the house into a bar, a bathroom and a game area.

That kind of job is building work as much as landscaping. Zach is our licensed residential builder, and our own team handles the plumbing, electrical and permits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What have you built on Isle of Palms?

A bright, coastal pool pavilion with a tabby fireplace and a travertine deck. For the same client we converted the area under their raised house into a bar, a bathroom and a game area.

Can you finish the space under a raised house?

Yes. On Isle of Palms we converted a client’s under-house area into a bar, bathroom and game area. Zach is a licensed residential builder, and our own team handles the plumbing, electrical and permits.

Does salt air change what you build with?

It matters. Salt air near the water corrodes cheap fixtures and fasteners, so materials are chosen with that in mind.

How much does a pool pavilion cost?

Pavilions run $30,000 to $90,000 or more. At $30,000 it is small and bare bones, and finishes like a fireplace, trim and a kitchen are what take it up.

Featured Projects

A few jobs we’re proud of

Pool Cabana · West Ashley

Built for parties and game days

The homeowner wanted a cabana by the pool for hosting parties and watching football. We built it with a rain screen behind the SmartSide siding, so moisture can drain and dry instead of sitting against the framing, and cedar posts and beams finished with craftsman trim.

The lighting is layered for use after dark. Tape lights hidden above the beams wash the open rafter ceiling, with wall sconces and bistro lights around them. Beyond the build quality, what impressed him most were the design ideas we brought to the project and the attention to detail.

See the project →

Pool Pavilion · Mount Pleasant

A resort feel at home

Large grand engineered beams let us space the rafters 24 inches apart for a grand timber-frame look. The pavilion has an outdoor kitchen, a shiplap TV wall and a bathroom, set on a travertine pool deck. The homeowners said it felt like something from a high-end resort.

See the project →

Fire & Water Feature · Mount Pleasant

A place to unwind

We built this fountain and fire feature for a Mount Pleasant wellness center, as a calming spot for their clients. It took masonry, plumbing and a gas hookup, all handled by our own crew.

Get in Touch

Let’s talk about your yard

Tell us a little about your yard and what you have in mind. Doug or Zach will get back to you to set up a consultation at your home. Prefer to talk? Call or text Doug at (843) 614-9773.

The consultation at your home is free around Charleston, Mount Pleasant and Summerville. For Kiawah, Seabrook, Wadmalaw and Awendaw, or anywhere else more than an hour from Charleston or Summerville, there’s a charge for the proposal trip.

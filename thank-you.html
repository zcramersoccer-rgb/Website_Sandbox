Thanks — we’ve got it

Home / Thank You

Your message is with us. Doug or Zach will get back to you personally — not a call centre, not a sales rep. They are the two who would design and build the job.

We are out on site most of the day, so if it’s easier to talk, or your question is quick, call or text Doug on (843) 614-9773. We are around Monday to Friday 8–5 and Saturday 9–2.

While you wait

If you have not already, it’s worth a look through our recent work — pergolas and pavilions, outdoor kitchens, patios, retaining walls and full backyard builds around Charleston, Mount Pleasant and Summerville. Each project page explains what was actually built and why.

It also helps to have a rough idea of budget before we meet. What an outdoor living space costs in Charleston sets out real ranges for the things people ask about most.

What happens next

  1. We talk. A quick call or message to understand what you are after and roughly what you want to spend.
  2. We come and look. Doug or Zach walks the property with you. The consultation at your home is free around Charleston, Mount Pleasant and Summerville. For Kiawah, Seabrook, Wadmalaw and Awendaw, or anywhere else more than an hour from Charleston or Summerville, there is a charge for the proposal trip — it comes off the price if you go ahead with the build, and it is not refundable if you do not.
  3. You get a design and a price. Drawn by the people who will build it.

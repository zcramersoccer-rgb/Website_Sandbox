Water Features You Hear a Fountain Before You See It

A fountain is the only thing we build that you hear before you see. It covers traffic noise, it gives a courtyard a center, and it is the reason people sit outside in a small yard that has nothing else going on.

We build them, plumb them and wire them ourselves, which matters more here than on most jobs: a water feature is masonry, plumbing and electrical in the same small structure, and it is the seam between those trades where leaks come from.

What a Water Feature Costs

Simple fountain kitfrom $1,000
Custom fountain, multiple layers or a built-in fire featureup to $30,000

Between those two ends sits most of what we build. Size, the number of levels, the finish and whether fire is involved are what move the number.

The fire and water feature we built for a Mount Pleasant wellness center is the upper end of that range: masonry, plumbing and a gas hookup in one structure, designed to give their clients somewhere calming to sit.

Poured Concrete, Not Block

This is the part worth understanding before you compare quotes.

When we build a custom fountain we pour solid concrete, then apply the finish on top — tile, stucco, tabby or whatever suits the house.

Fountains built from CMU block tend to have leakage problems, or more maintenance on the waterproofing membrane that is doing the work of holding water in. Block is faster and cheaper to build. A poured basin is the thing that is still holding water in ten years.

What It Takes to Keep One

Less than people fear. Cleaning it out once a year is the maintenance on our fountains.

That is the honest answer for a well-built feature on a proper basin. The horror stories people have heard almost always come back to a leaking structure rather than to fountains being inherently demanding.

How We Build Yours

  1. Free consultation. Where it goes, what you will hear from the house, and what it should look like.
  2. Design to suit the architecture — how many levels, what finish.
  3. Poured concrete structure, with the plumbing and any gas run by our own crew.
  4. Finish applied: tile, stucco, tabby or another finish.
  5. Set into the landscape, with the hardscape, planting and lighting around it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a fountain cost in Charleston?

A simple fountain kit starts around $1,000. A custom fountain with multiple layers, or one with a fire feature built in, can run up to $30,000.

How much maintenance is a water feature?

Ours need cleaning out once a year. That is the maintenance on a properly built fountain.

What is the best way to build a custom fountain?

We pour solid concrete and then apply a tile, stucco, tabby or other finish on top. Fountains built from CMU block tend to have leakage problems, or more maintenance on the waterproofing membrane.

Can you build a fire and water feature together?

Yes. We built one for a wellness center in Mount Pleasant, which took masonry, plumbing and a gas hookup, all handled by our own crew.

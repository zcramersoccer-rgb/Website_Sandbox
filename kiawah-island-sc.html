Local Expertise for Kiawah Island Landscapes

Kiawah Island's coastal environment creates landscaping challenges that require specialized knowledge. Salt air, sandy soils, high humidity, and strict community guidelines all shape what works — and what doesn't — on the island. At Cramers Landscaping, we bring deep Lowcountry expertise to every Kiawah Island project.

We know how to design for:

  • Salt air exposure and wind off the Atlantic
  • Sandy, fast-draining coastal soils
  • Native and salt-tolerant plant selection
  • Kiawah Island community and HOA guidelines
  • Coastal drainage and erosion management

Our work is tailored not just to your vision — but to Kiawah Island's unique coastal landscape character.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Do you provide free estimates on Kiawah Island?

      Yes. We offer free on-site consultations throughout Johns Island and the surrounding area.

      Are you licensed and insured?

      Yes. Cramers Landscaping is fully licensed and insured for all residential and commercial landscaping work in SC.

      Do you work within Kiawah Island's HOA and community guidelines?

      Yes. We are familiar with Kiawah Island's community standards and design our projects to meet or exceed those requirements.

      What plants work best on Kiawah Island?

      Salt-tolerant native species perform best in Kiawah Island's coastal environment. We recommend plantings like sea oats, yaupon holly, wax myrtle, muhly grass, and other Lowcountry natives that thrive in sandy soils and salt air.

      Can you maintain my property if I'm not on the island full-time?

      Absolutely. We are not a mowing company. For clients we have built large projects for, we come back to prune and shape the planting as it was designed to grow — keeping hedge lines where the plan put them and the layering readable as things fill in. round with regular scheduled visits.

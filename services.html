What We Build

Custom Outdoor Spaces, Designed and Built by One Family

Cramers Landscaping designs and builds complete outdoor spaces across Charleston and the Lowcountry, from the plantings and drainage you walk past every day to the patios, pergolas and outdoor kitchens you gather in. Every project is designed around your home and how you live, and Doug and Zach Cramer are on the job from the first site visit to the final walkthrough.

How a Project Works

Every project runs the same way, wherever you live. Doug or Zach comes out in person, and the people who design it are the people who build it. Local reviews, like an HOA or a town board, fit in before the permit, and we can prepare and submit those applications for you.

  1. On-site consultation, usually within a week of your call. We ask how you want to use the space and what budget you’re working with. There’s no point designing something you can’t afford.
  2. Budget, usually within a week of the consultation.
  3. Design: drawings matched to your home’s architecture, with optional 3D renderings for an additional cost. A larger project with a full drawn design carries a design fee, and what it comes to depends on the job. This takes 1 to 4 weeks depending on size. Small, simple jobs skip this step, pay nothing for design, and get a quote and plan right after the consultation.
  4. Final quote once the design is set.
  5. Permits and build. Structures like pergolas and pavilions need a permit, which takes about 4 to 6 weeks. A simple kitchen and pergola is built in 1 to 3 weeks. A pavilion takes 6 to 8 weeks.

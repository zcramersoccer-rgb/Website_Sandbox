Turf Earns Its Money Where Grass Keeps Failing

Artificial turf is not a replacement for every lawn. It is the answer for the specific places grass refuses to grow or refuses to survive: deep shade under live oaks, the strip of yard the dogs run every day, tight courtyards, poolside, and putting greens.

Done properly it is a construction job, not a carpet job. Almost everything that goes wrong with turf — ripples, low spots that hold water, edges that lift — traces back to what is underneath it, which is why we build the base the same way we build one for pavers.

What Artificial Turf Costs

Artificial turf, installedfrom $13 per sq ft

That price covers everything: base preparation and installation. Size, shape, the specific turf you choose and how much prep the yard needs are what move it.

Shape matters more than people expect. A simple rectangle cuts and seams efficiently; a curved yard with tree wells and bed lines takes more cutting, more edge work and more waste.

What Goes Underneath

We build the base from crush and run with granite fines on top. Crush and run compacts to a solid, stable platform; the fines screed to a true, smooth surface so the turf lies flat and drains instead of pooling.

Infill

Infill is what keeps the blades standing up and the turf sitting flat. We use sand, or antimicrobial sand where there are pets. If you have dogs, that is the version to get — it is the difference between turf you are happy with in year three and one you are not.

Living With It

  • It lasts 15 to 25 years, and longer depending on use and maintenance.
  • Yes, it gets hot. We will not pretend otherwise. On one project we installed a sprinkler system to spray the turf and cool it off, which works and is worth planning for if the area is in full sun and gets used barefoot.
  • Cleaning is a hose. That is the easiest way, and it is most of the job.
  • The real maintenance concern is dirt and leaf build-up in the turf. Washing and power brooming clears it out, and then you top up with some new sand if it needs it. Under oaks, that is an annual job rather than a never job.

How We Install It

  1. Free consultation and measurement, including how the area is actually used.
  2. Excavation and base. Crush and run compacted, granite fines screeded on top.
  3. Turf laid, cut and seamed to the shape of the space.
  4. Infill — sand, or antimicrobial sand for pets — then power broomed.
  5. Edges finished against beds, patios and pool decks.

Turf pairs with sod elsewhere in the same yard, with drainage where water is the underlying problem, and with planting around it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does artificial turf cost in Charleston?

From $13 per square foot installed, and that covers everything from base preparation through installation. Size, shape, the specific turf and how much prep the yard needs are what move the price.

How long does artificial turf last?

15 to 25 years, and longer than that depending on how hard it is used and how it is maintained.

Does artificial turf get hot?

It does. On one project we installed a sprinkler system to spray it and cool it off, which is worth planning for if the area is in full sun and will be used barefoot.

How do you clean artificial turf?

A hose is the easiest way and handles most of it. The main maintenance concern is dirt and leaf build-up in the turf, which washing and power brooming clears out. After that you can add some new sand if needed.

What goes under artificial turf?

Crush and run compacted as the base, with granite fines screeded on top so the surface is true and drains properly. Infill is sand, or antimicrobial sand where there are pets.

Is artificial turf good for dogs?

Yes, and it is one of the best uses for it. Use the antimicrobial sand infill rather than plain sand, and hose it down as part of normal cleaning.

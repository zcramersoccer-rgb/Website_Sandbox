Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the details you send us through this website. Last updated September 2026.

Who we are

Cramers Landscaping LLC is a family-run landscaping, hardscaping and outdoor structures company based in Summerville, South Carolina, serving Charleston and the Lowcountry. This policy covers cramerslandscaping.com and the chat assistant on it.

If you have a question about anything here, call or text Doug on (843) 614-9773 or email doug@cramerslandscaping.com.

What we collect, and when

We only collect what you type in. There is no account to create and nothing to sign up for.

When you fill in the consultation form: your first and last name, email address, phone number, the property address, and whatever you tell us about the project. Everything except the project description is required, because we cannot quote a yard we cannot find or call you back without a number.

When you use the chat: whatever you type. If you ask us to get in touch, the chat also collects your name, phone number, and your email address and project details if you give them.

Automatically: ordinary web analytics about which pages are visited, roughly where in the world the visit came from, and what kind of device it was. This is not tied to a name.

What we use it for

To answer you, to arrange and carry out a consultation, to quote and schedule work, and to keep records of the jobs we have done. Analytics we use to see which pages are useful and which are not.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who else handles it

Running a website means a handful of companies touch the data on our behalf. These are the ones that do:

  • Formspree delivers the form and chat enquiries to us by email.
  • Cloudflare serves the site and runs the chat assistant.
  • Hostinger hosts the website itself.
  • Google provides Analytics, which tells us how the site is used, and reCAPTCHA, which keeps spam out of the form. Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to those.

How long we keep it

Chat conversations are stored for 180 days and then deleted automatically. Phone numbers and email addresses are masked before a conversation is stored, so the stored copy does not contain them.

Enquiries that arrive by email, and the records of work we have done, we keep for as long as we are working together and afterwards for our own business records.

Cookies

The site sets a small number of cookies: some so the pages load quickly, and some for Google Analytics and reCAPTCHA. None of them are advertising cookies, and we do not use the site to follow you around the internet. Your browser can block or delete cookies; the site will still work.

Asking us to delete your details

Email doug@cramerslandscaping.com or call (843) 614-9773 and ask. We will delete what we hold, other than anything we have to keep for tax or warranty reasons, and we will tell you if that applies.

Children

This is a site for homeowners planning work on their property. It is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If we change how any of this works we will update this page and change the date at the top. Last updated September 2026.

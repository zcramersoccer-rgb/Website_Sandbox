Local & Family-Run

North Charleston Landscaping and Outdoor Living, Built for Family Backyards

Cramers Landscaping is a family company. Doug Cramer has spent more than 30 years building landscapes in the Lowcountry, and his son Zach is our licensed residential builder. Doug and Zach design every project themselves, and our own crew builds it.

The North Charleston projects on this page are all backyards: patios, kitchens, pergolas and fire pits. In the yard pictured here we built a bluestone patio, laid artificial turf and added a plant bed.

Two Stone Outdoor Kitchens

A porch addition with a stone kitchen

Here we added the porch itself and built a stone kitchen under it, with a bar top in black leathered granite, and a paver patio beside it.

A covered kitchen with a sink and burner

A different North Charleston job: a kitchen with a grill, sink, burner and fridge, with cabinets, a stone facade, a tile backsplash and a bar top. We added the structure overhead to protect the kitchen from rain.

Backyards Built Around the Fire Pit

A swing set around the fire

This one was all about kids and family hanging out around the fire pit: a large swing set on a paver patio, with a kit fire pit in the middle.

A pergola, a privacy wall and a fire pit

Another North Charleston backyard got a paver patio, a kit fire pit with a seating wall, and a pergola with a metal roof and a back privacy wall.

Frequently Asked Questions

What have you built in North Charleston?

Four of our North Charleston projects are on this page: a bluestone patio with artificial turf, a porch addition with a stone kitchen, a covered stone kitchen, and two family backyards built around kit fire pits.

How much does a fire pit cost?

A fire pit kit is about $1,000, and custom masonry starts from $2,500. Both North Charleston fire pits on this page are kits, set into paver patios.

Can you cover an outdoor kitchen that is already planned or built?

Yes. On one North Charleston kitchen we added the structure overhead specifically to protect the kitchen from rain.

Do you handle permits, gas and electrical?

Yes, all in-house. Roofed structures usually need a permit, which we pull ourselves, and our own team runs the gas, electrical and plumbing.
